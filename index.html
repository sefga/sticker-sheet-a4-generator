How Many Stickers Fit on an A4 Sheet?

Standard international A4 paper measures 210 × 297 mm. The formula calculates total copies based on margins and gaps:

Columns = ⌊(Usable Width + Gap) / (Sticker Width + Gap)⌋
Rows = ⌊(Usable Height + Gap) / (Sticker Height + Gap)⌋
Total Stickers = Columns × Rows

Print at Exact 1:1 Scale: Why 100% Matters

Desktop and mobile print dialogs often default to "Fit to Page" or "Shrink to Fit". This shrinks your document by 3–7%, distorting the physical millimeters. Always ensure "100%" or "Actual Size" is selected in your printer settings.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is StickerFit?

StickerFit is an online sticker sheet layout tool and PDF generator. It automatically arranges copies of a sticker on an A4 page using exact physical millimeter dimensions and prepares the layout for printing.

What size is an A4 sticker sheet?

International standard A4 paper measures exactly 210 × 297 mm (8.27 × 11.69 inches). StickerFit computes placement using these exact physical boundaries.

How do I ensure stickers print at their exact physical size?

Always choose "100%" or "Actual Size" in your print dialog. Never use "Fit to Page". You can also print our calibration test page to verify printer geometry with a physical ruler.

What resolution should my sticker artwork have?

For sharp, professional results, use artwork with at least 300 DPI. StickerFit dynamically calculates the effective DPI for your chosen dimensions and warns you if the resolution is too low.

Can StickerFit auto-rotate stickers to fit more copies?

Yes! When "Auto-rotate" is enabled, StickerFit compares both 0° and 90° orientations and automatically chooses whichever packs more stickers onto the sheet.

Are my uploaded images saved or sent to any server?

No. All image processing, cropping, and PDF layout rendering take place 100% locally in your web browser. Your artwork and images never leave your device.
